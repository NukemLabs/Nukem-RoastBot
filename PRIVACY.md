\# Nukem RoastBot Privacy Policy



\*\*Effective Date: September 16, 2026\*\*



Nukem RoastBot ("Nukem RoastBot," "the Bot," "we," "us," or "our") is a Discord application developed by NukemLabs.



This Privacy Policy explains what information Nukem RoastBot may access, process, store, and share when it is installed or used in a Discord server.



By installing or using Nukem RoastBot, you acknowledge the practices described in this policy.



\---



\## 1. Information Nukem RoastBot May Process



Nukem RoastBot may process limited information provided by Discord that is necessary to operate its features.



Depending on how the Bot is used, this may include:



\- Discord server IDs

\- Discord channel IDs

\- Discord user or member IDs

\- Discord usernames and display names

\- Server-specific configuration settings

\- Roast command interactions

\- Prefix command messages

\- Information necessary to determine automatic roast targets

\- Cooldown and timing information related to automatic roasting

\- Generated roast text

\- Generated roast card images



Nukem RoastBot does not require users to create a separate NukemLabs account.



\---



\## 2. Message Content



Nukem RoastBot may have access to Discord message content because it supports traditional prefix commands in addition to Discord slash commands.



Message content is used only as necessary to recognize and process supported bot commands and related functionality.



Nukem RoastBot is not designed to archive, sell, or build advertising profiles from general Discord conversations.



Server owners who do not want traditional message-based commands may choose to rely on slash commands instead.



\---



\## 3. Server Configuration Data



Nukem RoastBot stores server-specific settings so configuration can survive restarts and deployments.



Stored configuration may include:



\- Server ID

\- Selected automatic roast channel

\- Automatic roasting enabled or disabled state

\- Roast interval

\- Roast probability

\- Member roast cooldown information

\- Other settings required for Bot operation



This information may be stored in a persistent SQLite database hosted with the Bot.



\---



\## 4. Automatic Roasting



If automatic roasting is enabled by a server administrator, Nukem RoastBot may process server member information in order to select eligible roast targets.



This may include:



\- Discord member IDs

\- Display names

\- Server membership information

\- Previous roast timing or cooldown information



Automatic roasting is controlled by the server's configuration.



Server administrators are responsible for deciding whether automatic roasting is appropriate for their community.



\---



\## 5. AI Processing



Nukem RoastBot uses Google's Gemini API to generate roast content.



Information sent to the AI service may include limited information required to generate the requested roast, such as:



\- A Discord username or display name

\- Roast instructions

\- Context generated by Nukem RoastBot for the requested response



Nukem RoastBot does not intentionally send Discord authentication tokens, API keys, passwords, private deployment credentials, or other secret credentials to the AI provider.



Use of Google's services may also be subject to Google's own terms and privacy policies.



\---



\## 6. Generated Roast Cards



Nukem RoastBot may generate image files containing:



\- A Discord username or display name

\- AI-generated roast text

\- Nukem RoastBot visual artwork



Generated roast card images may be created temporarily on the hosting system so they can be uploaded to Discord.



Temporary generated images are not intended to be used for user profiling or advertising.



\---



\## 7. Hosting and Infrastructure



Nukem RoastBot may use third-party infrastructure and services to operate.



These may include:



\- Discord

\- Google Gemini

\- Railway

\- GitHub



These providers may process technical information according to their own privacy policies and service terms.



Railway may host the running Bot, persistent database, application logs, and temporary generated files.



GitHub may host the public source code and project documentation.



\---



\## 8. Application Logs



Nukem RoastBot and its hosting provider may create operational logs for purposes such as:



\- Diagnosing errors

\- Monitoring Bot availability

\- Debugging failed commands

\- Investigating deployment problems

\- Maintaining service reliability



Logs may contain technical identifiers or limited information associated with Bot activity.



Logs are not intended to be used for behavioral advertising.



\---



\## 9. Information We Do Not Intentionally Collect



Nukem RoastBot is not designed to intentionally collect:



\- Passwords

\- Payment card information

\- Government identification numbers

\- Precise physical location

\- Private authentication tokens

\- Discord account passwords

\- Biometric information

\- Personal advertising profiles



Users should never provide sensitive personal information to the Bot.



\---



\## 10. Data Retention



Server configuration data may be retained for as long as necessary to provide Nukem RoastBot functionality.



Temporary generated roast images may be deleted after they are sent to Discord or during normal application cleanup.



Operational logs may be retained by hosting providers for a limited period according to their own systems and policies.



If Nukem RoastBot is removed from a Discord server, some configuration records may remain until they are manually deleted, automatically cleaned up, or removed as part of maintenance.



\---



\## 11. Data Sharing



NukemLabs does not sell personal information collected through Nukem RoastBot.



Information may be processed or shared only when reasonably necessary to:



\- Operate the Bot

\- Generate AI responses

\- Deliver Discord functionality

\- Host the service

\- Diagnose technical problems

\- Comply with applicable legal obligations

\- Protect the security or integrity of the service



Third-party service providers may process information as necessary to provide their services.



\---



\## 12. Server Administrator Responsibility



Nukem RoastBot is intended for communities where roast-style humor is understood and welcome.



Server administrators are responsible for:



\- Deciding whether the Bot is appropriate for their server

\- Choosing where automatic roasting is enabled

\- Configuring roast frequency and probability

\- Managing access to Bot commands

\- Informing their community when appropriate

\- Removing or disabling the Bot if it is no longer wanted



\---



\## 13. Children



Nukem RoastBot is intended for use in accordance with Discord's age requirements and terms of service.



The Bot is not specifically directed toward children and is designed around adult-oriented roast humor that may include profanity.



\---



\## 14. Security



Reasonable efforts are made to protect the credentials and information required to operate Nukem RoastBot.



Private credentials such as Discord bot tokens and Gemini API keys are stored separately from the public source code.



No internet-connected system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.



\---



\## 15. Your Choices



Server administrators may stop Nukem RoastBot from processing new server activity by:



\- Disabling automatic roasting

\- Restricting the Bot's permissions

\- Removing the Bot from the server



Users may also choose not to invoke manual roast commands.



Requests relating to stored Nukem RoastBot configuration data may be submitted through the project contact method listed below.

This Privacy Policy may be updated as Nukem RoastBot changes.



Material changes may be reflected by updating this document and its effective date.



Continued use of Nukem RoastBot after an update constitutes acknowledgement of the revised policy.

\## 19. Summary



Nukem RoastBot processes only the information reasonably necessary to provide Discord roast functionality, server configuration, AI-generated responses, and custom roast cards.



The Bot is not designed to sell user information or operate as an advertising or tracking platform.



Server owners remain in control of whether Nukem RoastBot is installed and whether automatic roasting is enabled.
